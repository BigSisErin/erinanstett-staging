I’m a creative and design leader who has always believed curiosity is the best tool in the box. If something doesn’t exist yet, I’ll figure out how to build it. That’s been the through-line of my career (and honestly, my life).

We live more and more on screens, so I believe in making things people connect with. Things you want to reach out and touch: new made old and familiar, in any medium. I’m drawn to work that feels like it has a story, and I bring that to everything I make.

Away from the screen, I’m usually making by hand. Music, furniture, a home renovation, a rock wall: making runs through every part of my life, and it’s the driving force behind everything I do.

  • Creative direction
  • Brand identity, packaging design
  • Music design: album and single art, gig and tour posters, venue support
  • Brand merch
  • Design and build: spaces, interiors, events, experiences
  • Creative team consulting

Let’s chat

I’ve led creative teams, built a studio from scratch inside a fast-growing tech company, and worked both agency and brand side, helping ideas find their voice and form. I’m currently taking on freelance gigs as well as running my own Creative Studio, Big Sis.

Right now I’m running Big Sis Creative Studio with Jason Mann, collaborating with creatives, teams, brands and fellow visionaries on freelance and contract projects that mix branding, creative direction, design, storytelling, and a little bit of experimentation.

We find the soul of a brand and share it with the world. Grounded in experience, guided by instinct and dedicated to true collaboration, we deliver carefully crafted creative with efficiency and a whole bunch of passion, so brands show up with authenticity and stand out for all the right reasons. Big Sister energy, smart creative, no B.S.

We focus on working with female founders, community builders and mission-driven brands looking to make a real impact.
