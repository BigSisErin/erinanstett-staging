Erin: An Abridged Guide

Fig. 1. The operator.

Erin Anstett

I’m a creative and design leader who has always believed curiosity is the best tool in the box. If something doesn’t exist yet, I’ll figure out how to build it. That’s been the through-line of my career (and honestly, my life).

Operating history

I’ve led creative teams, built a studio from scratch inside a fast-growing tech company, and worked both agency and brand side, helping ideas find their voice and form. I’ve made plenty of things outside the office too. The medium changes, but the drive’s the same: to make something that connects.

Music is my boyfriend

It’s also how I found design. As a kid I flipped through my parents’ vinyl collection, fascinated by the art on the sleeves. When I started my own collection of vinyl, tapes and CDs, I was always enamored with the captivating work that came with them.

What I make

That’s where it started: objects you hold, flip over and read. I still make things people can touch.

In an increasingly digital world, people want something to connect with. Texture, weight, a little wear. I aim for work that feels familiar and new at the same time, like it has a backstory and has seen things.

Current setting

Big Sis Creative Studio

Right now I’m running Big Sis Creative Studio with Jason Mann, collaborating with creatives, teams, brands and fellow visionaries on freelance and contract projects that mix branding, creative direction, design, storytelling, and a little bit of experimentation.

I’ve got a soft spot for people- and mission-driven work, music-related projects, and brands brave enough to challenge the expected.
