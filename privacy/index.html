Privacy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

This site does not run analytics, ad trackers or cookies of its own, and it has no forms. I do not know who visits.

What stays on your device

If you turn the sound on or off, your choice is saved in your browser (local storage, key ea-sound) so it carries from page to page. It never leaves your device. Clear your site data and it is gone.

Videos

Project videos are embedded from Vimeo and YouTube, and they load as you browse a reel. Those services may set their own cookies or log your visit once a video loads, under their own policies. I ask Vimeo not to track (do-not-track mode) and use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, but I do not control what they do. Vimeo privacy · YouTube / Google privacy

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web host, may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service. I do not have access to those logs. GitHub privacy statement

Email

The “Let’s chat” links open your email app, addressed to erin@bigsiscreativestudio.com. If you write, I get your message and your address, and I use them only to reply. I do not add anyone to a list or sell or share contact details.

Links out

This site links to LinkedIn, Instagram and other sites. Once you click through, their policies apply, not mine.

Questions

Write to erin@bigsiscreativestudio.com.
