The Shitty Barn

Co-founder, producer and creative director

The Shitty Barn started as a handful of Spring Green friends who loved music, brewed beer, and were tired of white-knuckling it back from Madison shows at 2 a.m. As a co-founder, producer and creative director I helped shape the vision from day one: a homegrown, intimate listening space built from a raw agricultural barn. I led the entire visual and narrative identity, and designed and screen-printed the first posters in my basement. Fifteen years and over 350 shows later, the barn is still running strong.
