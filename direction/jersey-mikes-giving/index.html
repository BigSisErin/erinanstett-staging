Giving Campaigns/The Sub Abovers

Jersey Mike’s long-standing Day of Giving needed to go above and beyond. For social activation, we designed stickers that gave the brand’s loyal following a way to post that they showed up, generating a ton of organic UGC and awareness.

Alongside the general and Day of Giving TV spots, we concepted “The Abovers,” a program launched in 2018 that invited locals to nominate people making a difference in their communities. Winners received grants from Jersey Mike’s, and we captured and shared their stories to amplify their causes. The program continues today, a lasting expression of the brand’s belief that doing good is good business.
