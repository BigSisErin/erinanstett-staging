Fetch: The Studio

Creative direction and team leadership at Fetch

I built and led The Studio, Fetch’s in-house creative agency: a 13-person team spanning photography, production, animation, videography, illustration, merch and feature branding. The team also led internal merch design. From the ground up, I developed the team, the structure and a 2,000-square-foot photo and video studio that supported product, marketing, sales, media, PR and internal communications. The Studio became the brand’s creative engine. We collaborated closely with our national CPG and retail partners to create on-brand in-app content, alongside bespoke campaigns, shoots and motion content for Fetch, generating millions in quarterly revenue.

Fetch: The Studio

Before The Studio

Product design (2021 to 2023). I led the product design team behind a unified design system and The Pointlings, the characters that shaped how Fetch celebrates users in the app. Their integration into Snap Celebrations earned a U.S. patent, where I’m named as an inventor.

Rebrand (2020). As Design Director I led the creative development of the Fetch rebrand, uniting two separate logos into a single identity system, and helped grow the design team from three to twelve.
