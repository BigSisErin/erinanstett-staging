American Players Theatre

Creative and art direction at Planet Propaganda

My collaboration with the Tony Award® winning American Players Theatre spans more than a decade, beginning as a freelance designer and continuing through my years at Planet Propaganda, where I ran creative and art direction on their seasonal campaigns. It remains one of my favorite partnerships: translating the theatre’s spirit of place, performance and community into fresh visual storytelling year after year.

Art in the Woods

In 2019, I concepted and pitched “Art in the Woods,” evolving APT’s tagline, Play in the Woods, into a more immersive experience. Local visual art installations along the trails and gathering spaces of the theatre grounds connected performance and community.
